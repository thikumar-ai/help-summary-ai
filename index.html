      Audience

      This guide is for analysts and team leads who use Nimbus Workspace to monitor data, build reports, and share results.

      Getting Started

      Nimbus Workspace is a cloud workspace for collecting operational data, turning it into reports, and sharing insights with your team.

      You can connect data sources, monitor key metrics on dashboards, create scheduled reports, and ask the built-in Assistant questions in plain language.

        Signing in to Nimbus Workspace

        Note

        You need a Nimbus Workspace account. If you don't have one, contact your administrator.

        To sign in, do the following:

        1. Go to the Nimbus Workspace URL. The sign-in page is displayed.
        2. Enter your Email and Password.
        3. Click Sign in. The Home page is displayed.

        Tip

        Select Keep me signed in on a trusted device to skip this step next time.

        Key Features

        Nimbus Workspace offers the following key features:

        Data Connections
        Connect databases, spreadsheets, and cloud services, and refresh them on a schedule.
        Dashboards
        Monitor key metrics in real time with charts you can filter and drill into.
        Reports
        Create, schedule, and share reports in PDF or spreadsheet format.
        Alerts
        Get notified when a metric crosses a threshold you define.
        Assistant
        Ask questions in plain language and get answers, charts, and summaries.

        Reports

        The Reports area lets you create reports from your data, review them, and share them on a schedule.

        Each report combines one or more data sources, filters, and visuals. You can export reports or send them to recipients automatically.

          Viewing the Reports Page

          To open the Reports page, in the left navigation pane, click Reports. The page lists all reports you can access.

          The Reports page displays the following columns:

          ColumnDescription
          Report IDDisplays the unique identifier of the report. Click it to copy the ID.
          NameDisplays the report name. Click it to open the report.
          TypeDisplays the report type, such as Summary, Detail, or Trend.
          OwnerDisplays the person who created the report.
          StatusDisplays the report status: Draft, Scheduled, or Published.

          Searching for reports

          Type a name, owner, or keyword in the search bar, and then press Enter. Matching reports are shown with a relevance score.

          Note

          The Relevance column appears only after you search.

          Creating a Report

          You can create a report in either of these ways:

          • Creating a blank report
          • Creating a report from a template

          Creating a blank report

          1. In the left navigation pane, click Reports.
          2. Click + New report. The New Report page is displayed.
          3. Complete the following fields:
            FieldDescription
            NameEnter a name for the report.
            Data sourceSelect one or more connected data sources.
            TypeSelect Summary, Detail, or Trend.
            OwnerDisplays your name.

            Note

            The Owner field is filled in automatically.

          4. Click Create. The report is saved as a draft.

          Creating a report from a template

          1. On the Reports page, click Templates.
          2. Select a template, and then click Use template.
          3. Update the name and data source, and then click Create.

          Caution

          Changing the data source can remove visuals that use fields not available in the new source.

          Scheduling a report

          1. Open the report, and then click Schedule.
          2. Select the frequency and recipients.
          3. Click Save. The report status changes to Scheduled.

          Warning

          Recipients outside your organization receive the data in the report. Check the content before you schedule it.

          Assistant

          The Assistant answers questions about your data in plain language and can create charts and summaries for you.

            Asking the Assistant a Question

            Use the Assistant for quick answers without building a report.

            To ask a question, do the following:

            1. In the top bar, click Assistant. The Assistant panel opens.
            2. Select a Mode, type your question, and then press Enter.
            3. Review the answer. Ask a follow-up question to refine it.

            Sample prompts

            Select a prompt to copy it.

            Tip

            Be specific about the time period and metric for the most accurate answers.

            Dashboards

            Dashboards show your key metrics in one place, updated each time the data refreshes.

            Widget types

            WidgetDescription
            KPI cardShows a single metric with its change since the previous period.
            Line chartShows how a metric changes over time, by day, week, or month.
            Bar chartCompares a metric across categories, such as regions or products.
            TableLists records with sortable columns and inline filters.
            MapShows a metric by location, shaded by value.
            FunnelShows how many records move through each stage of a process.

            Adding a widget

            To add a widget to a dashboard, do the following:

            1. Open the dashboard, and then click Edit.
            2. Click + Add widget, and then select a widget type.
            3. Select the data source and metric, and then click Add.

            Sharing a dashboard

            To share a dashboard, do the following:

            1. Open the dashboard, and then click Share.
            2. Enter the names of the people or groups to share with.
            3. Select Can view or Can edit, and then click Send.

            Alerts

            Alerts notify you when a metric crosses a threshold that you define.

            Alert fields

            FieldDescription
            MetricSelect the metric that the alert watches.
            ConditionSelect whether the alert fires above or below the threshold.
            ThresholdEnter the value that triggers the alert.
            FrequencySelect how often the metric is checked, from every 5 minutes to daily.
            RecipientsSelect the people who receive the notification by email.

            Creating an alert

            To create an alert, do the following:

            1. In the left navigation pane, click Alerts.
            2. Click + New alert, and then complete the alert fields.
            3. Click Save. The alert starts checking the metric.

            Note

            Alerts pause automatically while their data source is refreshing.

            Glossary

            The following terms are used throughout Nimbus Workspace:

            Data source
            A database, spreadsheet, or service that Nimbus Workspace reads data from.
            Metric
            A calculated value, such as total orders or average response time.
            Dimension
            A field you group or filter by, such as region, product, or date.
            Refresh
            The process of loading the latest data from a data source.
            Workspace
            A shared area where a team keeps its dashboards, reports, and alerts.
            Owner
            The person who created an item and can change who has access to it.
            Schedule
            A rule that runs a report or refresh automatically at set times.
            Threshold
            The value that a metric must cross for an alert to fire.

            Data Connections

            Data Connections bring data into Nimbus Workspace from the systems your team already uses.

            Each connection stores the address, sign-in details, and refresh schedule for one data source. Dashboards, reports, and alerts read from these connections, so a connection must exist before you can build anything on top of it.

              Connecting a Data Source

              Nimbus Workspace supports the following data source types:

              Source typeDescription
              SQL databaseReads tables and views from SQL Server, PostgreSQL, or MySQL over an encrypted connection.
              SpreadsheetReads one or more sheets from an uploaded Excel or CSV file.
              Cloud storageReads files from a shared cloud folder and picks up new files automatically.
              REST APICalls a web service on a schedule and stores the returned records.

              To connect a data source, do the following:

              1. In the left navigation pane, click Data Connections.
              2. Click + New connection, and then select the Source type.
              3. Enter the Name, the server address or file, and the sign-in details.
              4. Click Test connection. A green check mark shows that Nimbus Workspace can reach the source.
              5. Click Save. The first data load starts automatically.

              Caution

              Use a read-only account for database connections. Nimbus Workspace never needs to change data in the source.

              Refreshing Data

              A refresh loads the latest records from a data source. You can refresh a connection on a schedule or on demand.

              To schedule a refresh, do the following:

              1. Open the connection, and then click Refresh settings.
              2. Select Hourly, Daily, or Weekly, and then set the start time.
              3. Click Save. The next refresh time appears on the connection card.

              Refresh status

              StatusDescription
              Up to dateThe last refresh finished successfully and no refresh is due.
              RefreshingA refresh is running. Alerts on this source pause until it finishes.
              FailedThe last refresh stopped with an error. Select the status to see the error message.
              PausedScheduled refreshes are turned off for this connection.

              Tip

              To refresh right away, open the connection and click Refresh now.

              Workspaces and Sharing

              A workspace is a shared area where a team keeps its dashboards, reports, alerts, and data connections.

              Every item belongs to one workspace. Members of the workspace get access based on their role, so you rarely need to share items one by one.

                Understanding Roles and Permissions

                Each workspace member has one of the following roles:

                RoleDescription
                ViewerOpens dashboards and reports but cannot change them or see data connection settings.
                ContributorCreates and edits dashboards, reports, and alerts in the workspace.
                ManagerAdds and removes members, changes roles, and manages data connections.
                OwnerHas full control, including renaming or deleting the workspace. Each workspace has at least one owner.

                Note

                Items shared directly with a person keep that access even if the person leaves the workspace.

                Inviting Workspace Members

                To invite people to a workspace, do the following:

                1. Open the workspace, and then click Members.
                2. Click Invite, and then enter one or more email addresses.
                3. Select a Role for the new members.
                4. Click Send invitations. Invited people get an email with a link that expires after 7 days.

                Tip

                Invite a group email address to give a whole team access at once.

                Account Settings

                Account Settings is where you manage your password, sign-in security, and how Nimbus Workspace notifies you.

                To open Account Settings, click your profile picture in the upper-right corner, and then click Account settings.

                  Changing Your Password

                  To change your password, do the following:

                  1. Open Account settings, and then click Security.
                  2. Click Change password.
                  3. Enter your current password, and then enter the new password twice.
                  4. Click Save. You stay signed in on this device and are signed out everywhere else.

                  Passwords must have at least 12 characters and include a number and a symbol.

                  Warning

                  Never share your password. Nimbus Workspace support never asks for it.

                  Setting Notification Preferences

                  You can choose how Nimbus Workspace tells you about the following events:

                  EventDescription
                  Alert firedSent when an alert you receive crosses its threshold.
                  Report deliveredSent when a scheduled report you own is delivered to its recipients.
                  Refresh failedSent when a data connection you manage fails to refresh.
                  Shared with youSent when someone shares a dashboard or report with you.

                  To change your notifications, do the following:

                  1. Open Account settings, and then click Notifications.
                  2. For each event, select Email, In-app, or Off.
                  3. Click Save.

                  Troubleshooting

                  Use this topic to fix the most common problems in Nimbus Workspace.

                  I can't sign in

                  Check that you are using your work email address. After five failed attempts, your account locks for 15 minutes. If you forgot your password, click Forgot password on the sign-in page to get a reset link.

                  My dashboard shows no data

                  Open Data Connections and check the refresh status of the source. If the status is Failed, select it to see the error, fix the sign-in details or address, and then click Refresh now. Also check that the dashboard date filter includes the period you expect.

                  A scheduled report was not delivered

                  Check that the report status is Scheduled and that each recipient address is correct. Reports are not sent while their data source is refreshing; delivery retries 30 minutes later.

                  An alert does not fire

                  Open the alert and check the condition and threshold. Alerts check the metric only at the selected frequency, so a short spike between checks is not detected. Alerts also pause while their data source refreshes.

                  Contacting support

                  If the problem continues, click Help in the top bar, and then click Contact support. Include the workspace name and the time the problem happened.

                    RP 01: Publishing a Weekly Sales Report

                    RP 01

                    Scenario

                    The sales team at Contoso Outdoor needs a weekly summary of orders by region, sent every Monday at 08:00.

                    As a team lead,

                    • I want to create a report from the sales data source.
                    • I want to review the figures before sharing them.
                    • I want the report sent to my team every week.

                    Features covered in this SOP

                    • Reports
                    • Templates
                    • Scheduling

                    Prerequisites

                    • Access to Nimbus Workspace
                    • A connected data source, for example, sales-orders

                    Procedure

                    Create the report

                    1. In the left navigation pane, click Reports.
                    2. Click Templates, select Weekly Summary, and then click Use template.
                    3. Name the report Weekly Sales, select the sales-orders data source, and then click Create.

                    Review the figures

                    1. Check the totals in the summary panel.

                      Example

                      RegionOrders
                      North1,284
                      South962
                      West1,105
                    2. Click Preview to see the report as recipients will.

                    Schedule the report

                    1. Click Schedule, select Weekly, Monday, and 08:00.
                    2. Add the sales team as recipients, and then click Save.
